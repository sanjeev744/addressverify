Why AddressVerify

Keep more orders deliverable without taxing checkout

AddressVerify checks once in checkout and again after purchase, then charges $0.02 for that unique Shopify order. You pick the action. Checkout stays fast unless you choose otherwise.

$0.02 once per unique order

One charge, even if you check twice

Checkout and post-checkout on the same order are one billable event. The Shopify Order ID is the key. Admin Test Address and provider outages are not billed.

A second chance after the order

Thank You and Order Status can suggest a fix, auto-correct, tag, or hold fulfillment after the customer already bought — without sending them back through checkout.

Your rules, not one blunt policy

Allow, Warn, Fix, Block, or Review for house numbers, PO Boxes, city and postal mismatches, and more. Start with Lite checks or turn on Full.

Fail open unless you close it

If a provider is down, AddressVerify fails over, then uses the built-in engine. Checkout is not blocked unless you turn on Fail closed.

No theme files to edit

Add the AddressVerify block in the Shopify checkout editor. Typical setup is about 15 minutes: Thank You, Order Status, optional Checkout, then save.

Fulfillment tools are included

Tag by error type, hold an order before the label is bought, send Klaviyo events, and watch errors, fixes, and estimated savings in the dashboard.
