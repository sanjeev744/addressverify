Pricing

Pay once per unique order

Your rate depends on store order volume: $0.40 (1–500), $0.30 (501–1,500), or $0.20 (1,501+). Charged once per unique Shopify order. Checkout and post-checkout on the same order are not charged twice. Test Address and provider errors are free.
