About

We build AddressVerify so more orders actually arrive

AddressVerify is made by Ekrocx Technologies. The product exists to cut failed shipments and support load for Shopify merchants — without locking you into a monthly plan you do not need.

What we optimize for

Deliverable addresses, predictable rules, and billing you can explain. $0.02 per unique order, charged once. Fail open unless you choose otherwise.

How we work with merchants

Setup is no-code. Support is email-first at support@addressverify.app. Higher-volume stores can ask for a guided rollout of Thank You, Order Status, tags, and holds.
